# Copywriting for Viral Growth

## Mike Ronny's Audience
- **Primary**: Gen Z (16-25), Myanmar + Southeast Asia
- **Platform**: Mobile-first, shares on TikTok, Instagram, Snapchat
- **Tone**: Casual, real, slightly edgy — not corporate
- **Language**: Simple English (easy for non-native speakers)

## Headline Formulas That Convert

```
[Curiosity gap]: "What do people REALLY think of you?"
[Social proof]: "Join 10,000+ people getting honest messages"
[Benefit-first]: "Get brutally honest feedback — anonymously"
[Fear of missing out]: "Everyone's using this. Are you?"
[Direct challenge]: "Ready to find out the truth?"
```

## CTA Button Copy

```
❌ Bad: "Submit" / "Click here" / "Sign up"
✅ Good: "Create My Free Whispr →"
✅ Good: "Get My Anonymous Link — Free"
✅ Good: "✨ Start in 30 Seconds"
✅ Good: "Find Out What They Think →"
```

## Social Proof Patterns
```
"Takes 30 seconds · No credit card"
"100% anonymous · They'll never know"
"Free forever · No spam"
```

## Project-Specific Copy

### Whispr
- Hero: "What do people *really* think of you?"
- Sub: "Create your free link. Share it. Get honest anonymous messages."
- CTA: "Create My Whispr → Free"
- Trust: "100% anonymous · 30 seconds setup · No credit card"

### DraftWin
- Hero: "Write Winning Proposals in 30 Seconds"
- Sub: "AI writes your freelance proposals. You win the clients."
- CTA: "Generate My Proposal — Free"
- Trust: "500+ freelancers · $2.4M in proposals generated"

## Rules
- Short sentences. Max 15 words per sentence.
- Never use: "leverage", "utilize", "synergy", "innovative solution"
- Always use: "free", "honest", "anonymous", "real", "seconds"
- Numbers are powerful: "30 seconds", "100%", "10,000+"
- Emoji adds personality — use sparingly (1-2 per section)
