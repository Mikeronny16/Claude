# Landing Page CRO (Conversion Rate Optimization) Skill

## Core Principle
For $2-$10 digital products: remove doubt, show value, create momentum.
The buyer has almost zero financial risk — the only barrier is uncertainty.

## Above-the-Fold Must-Haves
- Pain-first headline (not product-first)
- ONE clear CTA button (orange, high contrast)
- Social proof signal (counter, testimonials, or "as seen in")
- Price visible immediately (don't hide it)
- Mobile viewport: CTA must be visible without scrolling on iPhone 12 (390px)

## Social Proof Elements (ranked by impact)
1. Live download/purchase counter — "47 people downloaded this week"
2. Testimonials with name + country + specific outcome
3. "Featured in" logos (if any)
4. Reviewer star ratings
5. View counts / visitor numbers

## Product Preview Strategy
For digital downloads: ALWAYS show what they're getting
- Show actual sample content (3-5 items free, rest behind paywall)
- Product mockup image (Canva: show PDF cover, laptop screen, file folder)
- "What's inside" breakdown with specific counts per category

## Urgency Tactics (Honest Only)
- Real inventory limits ("47 of 100 spots at this price" — if true)
- Time-based price increase ("Price goes up after [real date]")
- NO fake countdown timers — destroys trust when users refresh
- Scarcity must be REAL or it backfires

## Price Anchoring
- Always show crossed-out original price
- Explain WHY it's discounted (launch price, early bird, limited)
- Position price against alternatives: "$2 vs 5 hours of trial and error"
- Never justify price by comparing to coffee (overused, feels cheap)

## CTA Button Best Practices
- Color: high contrast (orange on dark bg, never grey)
- Copy: action + benefit ("Get 105 Prompts Now" > "Buy")
- Size: minimum 48px height on mobile (touch target)
- Position: hero, after pain section, after pricing, sticky scroll bar
- Loading state: show spinner + "Redirecting..." (don't let it look broken)

## FAQ Section
Always include FAQ to handle objections:
- What exactly do I get?
- How do I access/download?
- Which [tools/AI/devices] does it work with?
- What if it doesn't work for me?
- Why is it [price]?
Answer each directly — don't be vague

## Page Structure That Converts
1. Hero (pain + CTA + social proof signal)
2. Stats bar (3 key numbers)
3. Pain points ("sound familiar?")
4. Solution / benefits
5. What's inside (with preview)
6. Compare table (with vs without)
7. Pricing (anchored, with features list)
8. Testimonials
9. FAQ
10. Final CTA
11. Footer

## Mobile-First Rules (iPhone 12, 390px)
- Font: 16px minimum body, 36px+ headlines
- Padding: 20px sides minimum
- No horizontal scroll
- Buttons: full-width on mobile
- Tables: stack vertically on mobile
- Images: max-width 100%
